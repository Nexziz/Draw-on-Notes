# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release. Mark up your notes by hand, right where you read them.

- **A pen on every note.** Click the pen icon at the top of a note to draw over the whole page, margins included. Click it again, or press Esc, to go back to reading.
- **A pen, a highlighter and an eraser.** The highlighter has a flat tip and lets the text show through, in light and dark themes. The eraser lifts off any stroke it touches, and so does the eraser end of a stylus.
- **Colour, size and transparency** for each tool, with seven colours and a picker for any other.
- **Undo and redo** every stroke, erase and clear.
- **Keep scrolling** with the mouse wheel while you draw. Your marks scroll with the note.
- **Shortcuts** while drawing: P, H and E for the tools, [ and ] for the size, Ctrl+Z to undo and Ctrl+Shift+Z to redo.
- **Your notes are never changed.** Drawings are kept in the plugin's own data and follow a note when you rename or move it.
